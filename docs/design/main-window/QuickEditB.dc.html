B. Inspector panel

A panel on the right edge that follows the selection, like the Keynote or Pages inspector on a Mac and a Settings page on Windows. Good for longer editing and on touch. Slint: plain layout, no popup, easy to build. Cost: it covers the right-hand columns while open (or the wall shrinks and carts move, which we ruled out), so it suits Edit mode, not a show.
