C. Native menu, then a sheet

Right-click gives the real system menu: the toggles as check marks, colours with swatch icons, outputs and groups as submenus. Edit Cart… opens a sheet (macOS) or dialog (Windows) for the label, gain, speed and fade. Slint: ContextMenuArea draws a genuine NSMenu or Win32 menu (muda), so this is the most native of the three. Cost: the label, gain and speed take two steps; menus cannot hold sliders or text fields.
